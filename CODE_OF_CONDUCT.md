# Code of Conduct

SimCam Lite is a small project built by developers for developers. We want everyone who reports a bug, asks a question
or sends a pull request to feel welcome, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Our standards

Do:

- be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to iOS development;
- assume good intent, and ask before concluding someone meant harm;
- give and accept feedback about code, not about the person who wrote it;
- respect that maintainers and contributors give their time for free;
- credit other people's work.

Don't:

- harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately;
- use sexualized language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances;
- make discriminatory jokes or comments;
- share someone's private information without their permission;
- keep pushing a point after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space, such as issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and whenever you
represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, email **support@ifateam.dev**. Reports are read only by the maintainers
and kept confidential. You won't face consequences for reporting in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review every report and respond in the way they find fair and proportionate. That can range from a
private conversation or a public warning to removing comments or code, or banning someone from the project temporarily
or permanently.
