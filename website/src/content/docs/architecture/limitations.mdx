SimCam Lite focuses on the most common iOS camera workflows—live previews, frame processing, QR/barcode scanning via video frames, photo capture, and audio data streams—while remaining honest about what isn’t implemented yet.

## What works today

- **Live preview (`AVCaptureVideoPreviewLayer`)**: SwiftUI and UIKit camera screens render live frames at 30 or 60 fps with proper aspect fill/fit and rotation.
- **Video frame processing (`AVCaptureVideoDataOutput`)**: Vision (`VNDetectBarcodesRequest`, `VNRecognizeTextRequest`), CoreML, WebRTC/video call SDKs, and custom Metal/CoreImage pipelines receive real `CMSampleBuffer` frames.
- **Photo capture (`AVCapturePhotoOutput`)**: Capturing stills returns full-resolution photos from the active back or front stream.
- **4K back camera format (`v0.2.5+`)**: Back cameras advertise a `3840 × 2160` format and respond to `.hd4K3840x2160` session presets.
- **Audio data output (`AVCaptureAudioDataOutput`)**: Supplies 48 kHz mono 16-bit PCM samples from the Mac’s microphone (or silence if mic permission is not granted).
- **Device discovery & rotation**: Emulates Triple (`0.5× · 1× · 3×`), Dual Wide, and Single camera topologies plus `AVCaptureDeviceRotationCoordinator`.

## Known limitations

<Callout type="warning" title="Unsupported AVFoundation APIs">
  The following features are not yet supported in SimCam Lite:
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Movie file recording (<code>AVCaptureMovieFileOutput</code>)</strong></li>
    <li><strong>Built-in metadata output (<code>AVCaptureMetadataOutput</code>)</strong> such as AVFoundation’s native QR/barcode object delegate</li>
    <li><strong>Multi-camera sessions (<code>AVCaptureMultiCamSession</code>)</strong></li>
  </ul>
</Callout>

### Notes on QR code scanning

If your app scans QR codes using **Apple Vision** (`VNDetectBarcodesRequest`) or another decoder operating on `AVCaptureVideoDataOutput` frames, QR scanning works out of the box. Apps relying exclusively on `AVCaptureMetadataOutput` will not receive metadata objects yet.

### Notes on 4K recording performance

Because the iOS Simulator has no hardware video encoder, recording 4K frames software-side saves fewer frames per second than a physical iPhone—though the captured dimensions (`3840 × 2160`) match real hardware.

## Found a missing API?

If your app uses an `AVFoundation` class or property that behaves differently on the Simulator, please [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/huyhunhngc/SimCamLite/issues) with the symbol or crash log.
